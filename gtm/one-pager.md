# FalconFleetOps

## Your robots are a spreadsheet. Your downtime is a number.

Mixed fleets hide in vendor portals. Downtime hides in an ops manager’s gut. FalconFleetOps gives 3PLs, e-commerce warehouses and RaaS financiers one accountable system for robot health, predictive maintenance, firmware assurance, repair execution and audit-ready proof.

### Why now

A 500-robot site does not need another portal. It needs the first cross-brand signal that a battery, bearing, encoder or firmware rollout is about to become a missed-pick event. FleetOps turns the hour before failure into a maintenance decision.

### Five pillars

| Pillar | What the operator gets |
| --- | --- |
| **Observe** | Normalized telemetry across MiR, Geekplus, Locus, Zebra, Omron, KUKA and custom AMRs. |
| **Predict** | Transparent rolling baselines, known failure signatures and remaining-useful-life estimates with evidence. |
| **Update** | Signed, staged OTA rollouts with health gates and one-click rollback. |
| **Fix** | Anomaly-created tickets, technician workflow and parts recommendations with supplier RFQ. |
| **Prove** | Hash-chained audit trails, firmware attestation, maintenance compliance and Insurer Packs. |
